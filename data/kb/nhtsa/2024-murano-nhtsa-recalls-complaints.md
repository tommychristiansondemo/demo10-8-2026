## 2024 Nissan Murano recalls

- **2024 Murano recall campaign 24V053000** (reported 2024-01-30) - component: Visibility:Glass, Side/Rear. Problem: Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2024 Murano vehicles. The incorrect primer may have been used on the quarter glass, resulting in the left and right quarter glass detaching from the vehicle. Risk: Detached glass panels can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. Remedy: Dealers will replace both side quarter glass panels, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 7, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PD102.
