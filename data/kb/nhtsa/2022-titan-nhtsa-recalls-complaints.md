## 2022 Nissan Titan recalls

- **2022 Titan recall campaign 22V772000** (reported 2022-10-13) - component: Back Over Prevention:Display Function. Problem: Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 Titan, Titan XD, Frontier, and 2021-2022 Rogue vehicles. The in-vehicle infotainment (IVI) system may continuously reboot when the vehicle is restarted, which can result in an inoperative rearview display. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility." Risk: An inoperative rearview camera display reduces the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash. Remedy: The IVI software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 18, 2022.
