## 2021 Nissan Kicks recalls

- **2021 Kicks recall campaign 23V496000** (reported 2023-07-18) - component: Steering:Electric Power Assist System. Problem: Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Versa and Kicks vehicles. An intermittent electrical connection in the electric power steering (EPS) torque sensor can disable the power steering assist. Risk: Loss of power steering assist can increase the steering effort required, especially at low speeds, and increase the risk of a crash. Remedy: Dealers will inspect the steering column torque sensor lot code and replace the steering column assembly, if necessary, free of charge.
