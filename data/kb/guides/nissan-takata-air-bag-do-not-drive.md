# URGENT: Takata air bag "Do Not Drive" warning for older Nissans

Source: NHTSA press release "Do Not Drive Warning: Nissan, Infiniti Takata" (nhtsa.gov, May 2024) and NHTSA recall
campaigns 20V-008 and 20V-747.

In May 2024 Nissan and NHTSA issued a **"Do Not Drive"** warning for about 84,000 older vehicles with **unrepaired**
Takata front-passenger air bag inflators:

- **2002-2006 Nissan Sentra**
- **2002-2004 Nissan Pathfinder**
- 2002-2003 INFINITI QX4

After long-term exposure to heat and humidity, the inflator propellant can degrade and the inflator can **explode when
the air bag deploys, spraying metal fragments** - this has killed and seriously injured people in the U.S.

What to do:
1. Stop driving the vehicle if it is one of the models above and the recall has not been repaired.
2. Check the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or nissanusa.com/recalls-vin.
3. Contact a Nissan dealer or Nissan Consumer Affairs (1-800-867-7669). The repair is **free**, and Nissan offers
   **free towing**, mobile repair, and in some locations a loaner vehicle.

Other model years and models were also part of the broader Takata recalls; any open air bag recall should be repaired immediately.
