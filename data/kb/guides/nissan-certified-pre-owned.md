# Nissan Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) coverage

Sources: Nissan Certified Pre-Owned program descriptions published by Nissan dealers and Kelley Blue Book /
Autotrader CPO program summaries (2025-2026). Program details can change; confirm on the vehicle's CPO documentation.

- **7-year / 100,000-mile Limited Warranty** (whichever comes first), measured from the vehicle's **original in-service
  date** (not the date you bought it used) - covers 600+ components including engine, transmission and drivetrain.
- **$50 deductible** per covered repair visit.
- **24/7 Roadside Assistance** during the CPO warranty period (flat-tire change with your good spare, jump start,
  lock-out assistance, towing).
- **Eligibility:** less than 6 years old from the original in-service date and fewer than 80,000 miles.
- **167+ point inspection** and reconditioning, CARFAX vehicle history report.
- Special APR financing may be available for eligible CPO vehicles.

Note: if the original 3-year/36,000-mile basic warranty is still active when the vehicle is certified, it continues to
apply first; the CPO limited warranty extends coverage beyond it. A CPO vehicle does not extend EV battery coverage
beyond the original 8-year/100,000-mile lithium-ion battery warranty.
