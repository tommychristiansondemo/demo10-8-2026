# Recalls, service campaigns, and technical service bulletins - what owners should know

- A **safety recall** is issued when a manufacturer or NHTSA determines a vehicle has a safety-related defect or does
  not comply with a federal safety standard. **Recall repairs are free** at any authorized Nissan dealer, regardless of
  vehicle age, mileage, or whether you are the original owner.
- **Check by VIN**: a recall for a model year does not always apply to every vehicle (it often covers a production
  date range). Check your 17-character VIN at **nhtsa.gov/recalls** or **nissanusa.com/recalls-vin**. The VIN is on the
  driver-side dashboard plate (visible through the windshield), the driver door-jamb label, your registration and insurance card.
- Owners are notified by first-class mail at the address on the vehicle registration; keep your address current, and
  sign up for NHTSA recall alerts.
- A **service campaign** / customer satisfaction program is a non-safety field action (for example a software update or
  extended coverage on a specific part). Nissan Consumer Affairs campaign line: 1-800-867-7669.
- A **technical service bulletin (TSB)** is repair guidance to dealers for a known condition; it is not a recall and is
  normally covered only while the vehicle is under warranty. NHTSA publishes manufacturer communications (TSBs) on nhtsa.gov.
- If you paid for a repair **before** a recall was announced, you may be eligible for reimbursement - ask the dealer or Nissan Consumer Affairs.
- To report a safety problem to the government, file a complaint at nhtsa.gov/report-a-safety-problem or call the
  NHTSA Vehicle Safety Hotline 1-888-327-4236.
