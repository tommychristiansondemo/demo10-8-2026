# Nissan CVT (continuously variable transmission) extended warranty programs

Nissan extended CVT / powertrain coverage for several model years as a result of class-action settlements.
Sources: BBB National Programs Nissan CVT class-action arbitration program pages (bbbprograms.org/programs/dr/class-action/nissan-cvt
and /nissan-cvt2), Nissan dealer service communications published by NHTSA, and public settlement coverage.
Coverage periods are counted from the vehicle's original in-service date, "whichever comes first".

## Program 1 - 2012-2017 models (settled 2019-2020)

| Vehicles | Original powertrain term | Extended term |
|---|---|---|
| 2013-2017 Nissan Sentra | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |
| 2012-2017 Nissan Versa | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |
| 2014-2017 Nissan Versa Note | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |
| 2013-2016 Nissan Altima | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |

## Program 2 - Rogue / Pathfinder (2014-2018)

Under a class-action settlement given final court approval on March 23, 2022, the CVT / powertrain warranty was extended
by 24 months / 24,000 miles - from 60 months / 60,000 miles to **84 months / 84,000 miles** - for **2014-2018 Nissan Rogue**
and **2015-2018 Nissan Pathfinder** (and 2014-2018 INFINITI QX60). Settlement benefits also included repair reimbursement
(up to $5,000 for qualifying repairs) or a voucher. Settlement information line: 1-855-786-1043.

## Program 3 - "Martinez" settlement (2017-2019 models)

| Vehicles | Original powertrain term | Extended term |
|---|---|---|
| 2017-2018 Nissan Altima | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |
| 2018-2019 Nissan Sentra | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |
| 2018-2019 Nissan Versa and Versa Note | 60 months / 60,000 miles | 84 months / 84,000 miles |

## What the extensions cover

The CVT assembly and sub-assemblies (belt and pulley), CVT control valve body, reprogramming of the transmission
control unit, CVT cooler kit where applicable, and the torque converter - the same parts as the original powertrain
coverage for the transmission. Settlements also provided reimbursement for qualifying CVT repairs paid by owners
between the end of the original powertrain warranty and the extended term (full reimbursement for repairs at a Nissan
dealer; capped, e.g. at $5,000, for repairs elsewhere), subject to claim deadlines that have since passed for some programs.

## Practical guidance for owners

- Because these programs end at 84 months, **most of them have now expired by age** (for example, a 2019 Sentra put
  in service in 2019 reached 84 months in 2026). Check your in-service date and mileage.
- Symptoms owners commonly report with CVTs: shudder or judder at low speed, hesitation or "rubber-band" feel,
  whining noise, sudden loss of power or "limp mode" (fail-safe), and the warning light/message. Have a Nissan dealer
  diagnose with CONSULT; software reprogramming and CVT fluid replacement with **Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid NS-2 / NS-3**
  (as specified for your vehicle) are typical first steps.
- Use only the CVT fluid specified in your Owner's Manual. Non-specified fluid can damage the CVT and is a warranty exclusion.
- Disputes about these extended warranties are handled through **BBB AUTO LINE** (1-800-246-2808), as provided in the settlements.
