# Security+Plus Extended Protection Plans (Nissan-backed service contracts)

Source: Nissan USA Warranty Information Booklets (Security+Plus section), 2024-2025.

- Security+Plus plans are optional extended protection plans (service contracts) created exclusively for Nissan owners
  and backed by Nissan, honored at Nissan dealers nationwide.
- If a covered component fails during the plan term, Nissan pays for the repair. Plans include 24-hour Roadside
  Assistance, towing, car rental and trip-interruption benefits, and lock in today's parts and service rates.
- They extend protection after the factory warranty (3 yr/36k basic, 5 yr/60k powertrain) expires. Coverage levels,
  terms and deductibles vary by plan; the Security+Plus agreement is the controlling document.
- Purchase through a Nissan dealer or online at nissan-securityplus.com (specialists Mon-Fri 7 AM-8 PM CST, Sat 8 AM-4 PM CST).
- A Security+Plus plan is a service contract, not a warranty; it is separate from recall repairs, which are always free.
