# Nissan New Vehicle Limited Warranty (USA) - coverage summary

Curated from the public Nissan USA Warranty Information Booklets (2023-2025 LEAF and TITAN booklets, nissanusa.com) and
Nissan USA published coverage statements. The booklet for the specific vehicle is the controlling document; this summary
is for orientation. All coverage periods are "whichever comes first" and begin on the **in-service date**: the date the
vehicle is delivered to the first retail buyer or put into use (for example as a dealer demo or company car), whichever is earlier.

## Coverage at a glance - gasoline passenger cars, SUVs and Frontier (current model years)

| Coverage | Term |
|---|---|
| Basic ("bumper-to-bumper") coverage | 36 months / 36,000 miles |
| Powertrain coverage | 60 months / 60,000 miles |
| Corrosion coverage (perforation / rust-through) | 60 months / unlimited miles |
| Seat belt limited warranty | 120 months / unlimited miles |
| Federal emission performance warranty | 24 months / 24,000 miles |
| Federal emission defect warranty | 36 months / 36,000 miles |
| Federal long-term emission defect (specified major parts, e.g. catalytic converter, engine control module, onboard diagnostic device) | 96 months / 80,000 miles |
| California emission performance and defect warranties (CA and states that follow CARB) | 36 months / 50,000 miles |
| California long-term emission defect (high-priced parts) | 84 months / 70,000 miles |
| Adjustment coverage (wheel alignment, wheel balancing, etc.) | 12 months / 12,000 miles |
| Refrigerant (A/C) recharge not associated with a warranted repair | 12 months, any mileage |
| Intelligent Key (I-key) and key fob batteries | 12 months of ownership |
| Original-equipment wiper blade inserts | 6 months of ownership |
| Corrosion of outer trim parts (moldings) | 12 months / 12,500 miles |
| Nissan Roadside Assistance (not a warranty; a service included with purchase) | 36 months / 36,000 miles |

## Electric vehicles (LEAF, ARIYA) - 2025 New Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty

| Coverage | Term |
|---|---|
| Basic coverage | 36 months / 36,000 miles |
| Powertrain coverage | 60 months / 60,000 miles |
| EV system coverage (electric motor, inverter, charger, etc.) | 60 months / 60,000 miles |

| Lithium-ion battery **capacity** coverage | 96 months / 100,000 miles - covers capacity loss below 9 bars (of 12) on the LEAF battery capacity level gauge; repair/replacement restores capacity to at least 9 bars, it does not guarantee a new-battery level |

| Seat belts | 120 months / unlimited miles |

Gradual capacity loss with time and use is expected and is **not** covered except as described under Lithium-ion
Battery Capacity Coverage. EV roadside and EV Consumer Affairs number: 1-877-NO GAS EV (1-877-664-2738).

## What powertrain coverage includes

- **Engine:** cylinder heads and block and all internal parts, rocker covers and oil pan, valve train and front cover,
  timing chain and tensioner, oil pump, water pump and fuel pump, fuel injectors, intake and exhaust manifolds
  (and supercharger/turbocharger where equipped), flywheel, seals and gaskets.
- **Transmission and transaxle (including the CVT):** case and all internal parts, torque converter and housing,
  automatic transmission control module, transfer case and internal parts, seals and gaskets, clutch cover and housing,
  A/T cooler, electronic transmission controls.
- **Drivetrain:** drive shafts, final drive housing and all internal parts, propeller shafts, universal joints, bearings, seals and gaskets.
- **Restraint system:** air bags and related electronic control systems.

## What is NOT covered (summary of general exclusions)

- Normal maintenance: oil and filters, wiper inserts (after 6 months), wheel alignment/balancing (after 12 mo/12k mi),
  headlight aiming, lubricants, coolant, worn brake pads/shoes/rotors/drums and clutch discs.
- Normal wear and tear, dings, dents, chips, scratches; stone chips, acid rain, tree sap, salt, hail, windstorm, flood and other environmental damage.
- Damage from accidents, theft, vandalism, fire, flooding (including engine water ingestion), racing, misuse such as towing beyond rated capacity.
- Damage caused by alteration, tampering, non-Nissan-approved accessories, improper installation, or improper repairs.
- Damage caused by lack of maintenance, or by fuel/fluids that do not meet the Owner's Manual specification.
- Glass breakage unless caused by a defect; damage from automated car washes or pressure washers.
- Embedded software/firmware is licensed, not sold (but repairs needed because of a Nissan over-the-air update are covered during the warranty).
- Vehicles with a salvage, junk, rebuilt or flood title, or declared a total loss, have **no** warranty coverage.
- Vehicles with an altered/replaced odometer where actual mileage cannot be determined.
- Tires are warranted separately by the tire manufacturer (see the tire warranty section of the booklet).

## Maintenance records and warranty

The warranty requires the vehicle to be maintained as described in the Owner's Manual. Keep receipts/records for all
maintenance; they may be requested for a related warranty repair and should be passed to subsequent owners. Maintenance
does not have to be done at a Nissan dealer to keep the warranty (keep the records), but **warranty repairs** must be
performed by an authorized Nissan dealer in the United States or Canada.

## Transferability

New-vehicle warranty coverage stays with the vehicle: subsequent owners receive the remaining time/mileage.
Roadside Assistance also transfers for the time remaining.

## Towing

If the vehicle is inoperative because a warranted part failed, towing to the nearest authorized Nissan dealer is
covered during any of the New Vehicle Limited Warranties (including emission and seat belt warranties).

## How to get warranty service or help

1. Contact any authorized Nissan dealer (dealer locator at nissanusa.com).
2. If the dealer cannot resolve it: Nissan Consumer Affairs, 1-800-NISSAN-1 (1-800-647-7261).
3. Questions about an open recall/campaign: Nissan Consumer Affairs campaign line 1-800-867-7669.
4. If still unresolved: the BBB AUTO LINE dispute-resolution program (information via 1-800-NISSAN-1).
