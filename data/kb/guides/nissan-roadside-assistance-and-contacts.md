# Nissan Roadside Assistance and important phone numbers (USA)

Source: Nissan USA Warranty Information Booklets and Owner's Manuals (2023-2025).

## Roadside Assistance (included with new Nissan purchase)
- Available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, in all 50 states, for **36 months / 36,000 miles** on new gasoline Nissans
  (TITAN: 60 months / 100,000 miles towing coverage per its booklet; LEAF: towing to the nearest certified EV dealer for
  60 months / 60,000 miles).
- Covers mechanical breakdown, tire failure (change to your good spare), lock-out service, jump starts, out-of-gas / out-of-charge
  assistance, and towing to the nearest Nissan dealer. Accident/collision tows are one-way. Charges for keys or non-warranty
  repairs are the owner's responsibility.
- Trip-interruption, alternate transportation, hotel/airline information and other concierge-style help are available through the same line.
- Benefits transfer to the next owner for the time remaining.
- **How to call:** gasoline vehicles 1-800-NISSAN-1 (1-800-647-7261); LEAF/EV 1-877-NO GAS EV (1-877-664-2738).
  Have ready: your name, VIN (driver-side dashboard plate), location, and the nature of the problem.
- Certified Pre-Owned vehicles have roadside assistance for the CPO warranty term.

## Escalation path for a warranty concern
1. Talk to the dealer's service manager, then the dealership general manager.
2. Call Nissan Consumer Affairs with your VIN, mileage, dealer name, and repair-order numbers.
3. If still unresolved, request BBB AUTO LINE arbitration (free to the owner; the decision is binding on Nissan if you accept it).
4. Many states have "lemon laws" for new vehicles with repeated unrepaired defects; check your state attorney general's website.
