# Nissan maintenance basics: schedules, oil life reminders, severe service

Source: 2023-2025 Nissan USA Owner's Manuals, chapter "Maintenance and schedules".

## Two schedules: standard and severe
Nissan provides standard maintenance and additional "severe use" maintenance. Follow the **severe** intervals if you
mainly drive under any of these conditions:
- Repeated short trips of less than 5 miles (8 km), or less than 10 miles (16 km) with outside temperatures below freezing.
- Hot weather in stop-and-go "rush hour" traffic.
- Extensive idling and/or low-speed driving for long distances (police, taxi, door-to-door delivery).
- Dusty conditions; rough, muddy or salt-spread roads.
- Towing a trailer, using a camper or a car-top carrier.
- Frequent braking or mountainous areas; sustained high-speed driving.

## Oil change reminders
Most 2021+ Nissans use an oil change reminder or an **Oil Control System (OCS)** that calculates the oil and filter
interval from driving conditions. When the oil replacement indicator appears, change the oil and filter **within two
weeks or 500 miles (800 km)**. If the OCS reminder resets early or stops working, change oil within 6 months or 3,750
miles of the last change. Typical published maximum intervals range from 5,000 miles/6 months (some engines/severe use)
to 7,500-10,000 miles/12 months (standard use) - always use the interval for your exact model and engine.
Use the oil viscosity and grade in your Owner's Manual (most current Nissan gasoline engines specify **0W-20** synthetic;
VC-Turbo engines specify Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent GF-6A).

## Items that commonly appear in Nissan schedules
- Tire rotation: every 5,000-7,500 miles on most models (some 10,000) - see your schedule.
- In-cabin microfilter: typically every 15,000 miles (more often in dusty or polluted areas).
- Engine air cleaner filter: typically every 30,000 miles (more often in dust).
- Brake fluid: replaced at fixed intervals (e.g., every 2 years or so; varies by model/schedule).
- CVT fluid: **inspect** at regular intervals; under severe use many models call for replacement around every 60,000 miles,
  or the dealer can read fluid-deterioration data with CONSULT. Only Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid (NS-2 or NS-3 as specified).
- Spark plugs (iridium): typically 105,000 miles for naturally aspirated engines; the 1.5L VC-Turbo three-cylinder (2024+ Rogue) is 60,000 miles.
- Engine coolant (Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant, blue): first replacement typically 105,000 miles / 84 months,
  then every 75,000 miles / 60 months.
- Intelligent Key battery: inspect/replace periodically (typically CR2032 coin cell).
- Fuel filter: in-tank, no periodic maintenance.

## Warranty and records
You may have maintenance performed by any qualified shop, but keep receipts - Nissan may request proof of maintenance for
related warranty repairs. Using fluids that don't meet Nissan's specification is a warranty exclusion.
