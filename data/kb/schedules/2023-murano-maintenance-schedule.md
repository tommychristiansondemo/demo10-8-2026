# 2023 Nissan Murano maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2023 Nissan Murano Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/murano/2023/2023-nissan-murano-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- VQ35DE: 3.5L V6 gasoline engine

## Oil change reminder

The manual references an oil and oil filter maintenance reminder that can be reset via the Vehicle Information Display (P. 2-18) after an oil change, but no details of an algorithmic oil life monitoring system are described in the provided pages.

## 2023 Murano fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP (or equivalent). If not available, synthetic 0W-20 GF-6 SP motor oil may be used. Must meet API certification or ILSAC certification and SAE viscosity standard. | 5-1/8 qt with oil filter change; 4-3/4 qt without oil filter change | VQ35DE |
| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. 50%/50% mixture with water. Provides antifreeze protection to -34°F (-37°C). | 2-1/4 gal with reservoir | all |
| CVT fluid | Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid NS-3 (or equivalent). Do not mix with other fluids. Do not use ATF or manual transmission fluid. | - | all |
| Differential gear oil | Genuine NISSAN Differential Oil Hypoid Super GL-5 80W-90 or equivalent conventional (non-synthetic) oil. | - | AWD models |
| Transfer case oil | Genuine NISSAN Differential Oil Hypoid Super GL-5 80W-90 or equivalent conventional (non-synthetic) oil. | - | AWD models |
| Power Steering Fluid (PSF) | Genuine NISSAN E-PSF or equivalent. Use of a power steering fluid other than Genuine NISSAN E-PSF may prevent the power steering system from operating properly. | - | all |
| Fuel | Unleaded regular gasoline with an octane rating of at least 87 AKI (Anti-Knock Index) / Research octane number 91. Do not use fuel containing more than 15% ethanol. Do not use leaded gasoline. Do not use fuel containing MMT. | 19 gal | all |
