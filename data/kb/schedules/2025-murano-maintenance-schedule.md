## Powertrains

- KR20DDET: 2.0L 4-cylinder turbocharged engine

## Oil change reminder

The vehicle is equipped with an Oil Control System (OCS) that calculates engine oil and filter change intervals based on driving conditions. Light conditions (freeway/city driving, no towing, no extended idling) result in normal display timing. Extreme conditions (repeated short trips under 5 miles, short trips under 10 miles in freezing temps, hot weather stop-and-go traffic, extensive idling, towing, frequent braking/mountainous driving, sustained high speed) result in early display timing. If the oil replacement indicator is displayed, change the engine oil within two weeks or less than 500 miles (800 km). If the indicator resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, change the oil within six months or 3,750 miles (6,000 km) from the last oil change.

## Severe driving conditions

- Repeated short trips of less than 5 miles (8 km)
- Repeated short trips of less than 10 miles (16 km) with outside temperatures remaining below freezing
- Operating in hot weather in stop-and-go 'rush hour' traffic
- Extensive idling and/or low speed driving for long distances, such as police, taxi or door-to-door delivery use
- Driving in dusty conditions
- Driving on rough, muddy or salt spread roads
- Towing a trailer, using a camper or using a car-top carrier
