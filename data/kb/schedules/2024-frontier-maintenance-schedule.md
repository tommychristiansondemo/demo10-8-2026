## 2024 Frontier fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP (or equivalent). If not available, synthetic 0W-20 SP GF-6A motor oil may be used. Must meet API certification or ILSAC certification and SAE viscosity standard. | 5-1/4 qt with oil filter change; 5 qt without oil filter change | VQ38DD |
| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. 50% antifreeze / 50% water. Provides antifreeze protection to -34°F (-37°C). | 3-1/8 gal (with reservoir) | VQ38DD |
| Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) | Genuine NISSAN Matic P ATF or equivalent. Do not mix with other fluids. | - | VQ38DD |
| Transfer fluid | Genuine NISSAN ATF D3M. Using fluid other than Genuine NISSAN ATF D3M may cause deterioration in driveability and transfer durability. | 1-5/8 qt | VQ38DD |
| Fuel | Unleaded regular gasoline with an octane rating of at least 87 AKI (Anti-Knock Index) / Research octane number 91. Do not use fuel containing more than 15% ethanol. | 21-1/8 gal | all |

## Severe driving conditions

- Driving in dusty conditions (air cleaner filter may need more frequent replacement)
- Vehicle immersed in water (propeller shaft must be re-greased after immersion)
- Severe use operating conditions as defined by Nissan requiring 5,000 mi / 6-month oil change intervals and more frequent brake, steering, suspension, drivetrain inspections
