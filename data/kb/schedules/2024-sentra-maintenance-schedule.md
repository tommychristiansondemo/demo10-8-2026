# 2024 Nissan Sentra maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2024 Nissan Sentra Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/sentra/2024/2024-nissan-sentra-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- MR20DD: 2.0L 4-cylinder

## Oil change reminder

The vehicle is equipped with an oil and oil filter maintenance reminder and oil control system. If the oil replacement indicator is displayed, change the engine oil and filter within two weeks or less than 500 miles (800 km). The system must be reset after each oil and filter change via the Vehicle Information Display (4.2-inch Type A or 7-inch Type B).

## Severe driving conditions

- Driving for prolonged periods in dusty conditions (affects air cleaner filter and in-cabin microfilter replacement frequency)
- Severe driving conditions requiring more frequent inspection of steering gear & linkage, axle & suspension parts, and drive shaft boots
