# 2023 Nissan Titan maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2023 Nissan Titan Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/titan/2023/2023-nissan-titan-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- VK56VD: 5.6L 8-cylinder gasoline engine

## Oil change reminder

No oil life monitor described in the provided pages. Oil and filter replacement is on a fixed mileage/time interval (every 7,500 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first).

## Severe driving conditions

- Towing a trailer
- Using a camper or car-top carrier
- Driving on rough or muddy roads
- Prolonged driving in dusty conditions
