# 2024 Nissan Titan maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2024 Nissan Titan Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/titan/2024/2024-nissan-titan-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- VK56VD: 5.6L 8-cylinder gasoline engine

## Oil change reminder

No oil life monitor described in the provided pages. Oil and filter replacement intervals are time/mileage based as published in the maintenance schedule.

## 2024 Titan fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP (or equivalent); synthetic 0W-20 GF-6A SP if above not available. Must meet API certification or ILSAC certification. | 6-7/8 qt with oil filter change; 6-1/2 qt without oil filter change | VK56VD |
| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; 50% antifreeze / 50% water; antifreeze protection to -34°F (-37°C) | 3-1/2 gal (Non-XD model, with reservoir); 3-1/2 gal (XD model, with reservoir) | all |
| Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) | Genuine NISSAN Matic P ATF or equivalent. Do not mix with other fluids. | — | all |
| Differential gear oil (front) | Genuine NISSAN Differential Oil Synthetic GL-5 75W-85. Do not mix with other fluids. | 2-5/8 pt (Non-XD model); 3-1/4 pt (XD model) | all |
| Differential gear oil (rear) | Genuine NISSAN Differential Oil Synthetic GL-5 75W-85. Do not mix with other fluids. | 4-7/8 pt (Non-XD model); 5-1/2 pt (XD model) | all |
| Fuel | Unleaded premium gasoline, minimum 91 AKI octane rating recommended. Unleaded regular 87 AKI minimum acceptable. No E-15 or E-85. No MMT-containing fuel. | 26 gal | VK56VD |

## Severe driving conditions

- Towing a trailer
- Using a camper or car-top carrier
- Driving on rough or muddy roads
- Prolonged driving in dusty conditions (affects air cleaner filter and in-cabin microfilter replacement frequency)
