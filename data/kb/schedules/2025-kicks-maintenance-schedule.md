# 2025 Nissan Kicks maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2025 Nissan Kicks Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/kicks/2025/2025-nissan-kicks-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- MR20DD: 2.0L 4-cylinder

## Oil change reminder

An oil replacement indicator is displayed when an oil change is due. If the indicator is displayed, change the engine oil and filter as soon as possible, then reset the display.

## 2025 Kicks fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP (or equivalent). If not available, synthetic 0W-20 GF-6A SP motor oil may be used. Must meet API or ILSAC certification. | 4-3/8 qt with oil filter change; 4 qt without oil filter change | MR20DD |
| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. 50% antifreeze / 50% water mixture providing protection to -34°F (-37°C). | 2 gal (with reservoir) | all |
| CVT fluid | Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent. Do not mix with other fluids. Do not use ATF or manual transmission fluid. | - | MR20DD |
| Fuel | See 'Fuel recommendation' (P. 630) in owner's manual. | 11-7/8 gal (2WD models); 12-3/8 gal (AWD models) | all |
| Brake fluid | See 'Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities' (P. 628) in owner's manual. | - | all |

## Severe driving conditions

- Prolonged periods of driving in dusty conditions (affects air cleaner filter and in-cabin microfilter replacement frequency)
- Severe driving conditions affecting steering gear and linkage, axle and suspension parts, drive shaft boots (inspect more frequently)
