# 2024 Nissan Murano maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2024 Nissan Murano Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/murano/2024/2024-nissan-murano-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- VQ35DE: 3.5L V6 6-cylinder

## Oil change reminder

An oil and oil filter maintenance reminder is displayed in the vehicle information display. It must be reset after each oil and filter change.

## 2024 Murano fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP (or equivalent synthetic 0W-20 GF-6A SP). Must meet API certification or ILSAC certification. | 5-1/8 qt with oil filter change; 4-3/4 qt without oil filter change | VQ35DE |
| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. 50/50 mixture providing antifreeze protection to -34°F (-37°C). | 2-1/4 gal with reservoir | VQ35DE |
| CVT fluid | Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid NS-3 only. Do not mix with other fluids. Do not use ATF or manual transmission fluid. | - | VQ35DE |
| Differential gear oil | Genuine NISSAN Differential Oil Hypoid Super GL-5 80W-90 or equivalent conventional (non-synthetic) oil. | - | AWD models |
| Transfer oil | Not specified in provided pages beyond capacity listing. | - | AWD models |
| Power Steering Fluid (PSF) | Genuine NISSAN E-PSF or equivalent. Use of other power steering fluid may prevent proper operation. | - | all |
| Fuel | Unleaded regular gasoline with octane rating of at least 87 AKI (Research octane number 91). Maximum 15% ethanol. Do not use fuel containing MMT. | 19 gal | all |

## Severe driving conditions

- Repeated short trips of less than 5 miles (implied by 5,000-mile severe oil change interval)
- Driving in dusty conditions (air cleaner filter and in-cabin microfilter require more frequent replacement)
- Severe operating conditions as defined by Nissan requiring the separate severe use maintenance schedule
