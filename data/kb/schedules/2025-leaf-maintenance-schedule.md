## 2025 Leaf fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooling system coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent | 5-5/8 qt (with reservoir); reservoir alone: 1/2 qt | all |
| Reduction gear fluid | Genuine NISSAN Matic S ATF only; do not mix with other fluids | 1-1/2 qt | all |
| Brake fluid | Genuine NISSAN Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3 | - | all |
| Multi-purpose grease | NLGI No. 2 (Lithium soap base) | - | all |
| Air conditioning system refrigerant | HFO-1234yf (R-1234yf) | - | all |
| Air conditioning system lubricant | NISSAN A/C System Oil ND-OIL11 or equivalent | - | all |
| Windshield washer fluid | Genuine NISSAN Windshield Washer Concentrate Cleaner & Antifreeze fluid or equivalent | 5/8 gal (US) | all |
