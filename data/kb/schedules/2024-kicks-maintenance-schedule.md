# 2024 Nissan Kicks maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2024 Nissan Kicks Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/kicks/2024/2024-nissan-kicks-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- HR16DE: 1.6L 4-cylinder

## Oil change reminder

No oil life monitor described in the provided pages. The schedule specifies fixed mileage/time intervals (every 7,500 miles / 6 months standard; every 5,000 miles / 6 months severe).

## 2024 Kicks fluids and capacities

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | Genuine NISSAN Motor Oil 0W-20 SP (or equivalent). If not available, synthetic 0W-20 GF-6A SP motor oil may be used. Must meet API certification or ILSAC certification. | 4-3/8 qt with oil filter change; 4 qt without oil filter change | HR16DE |
| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. 50% antifreeze / 50% water. Provides antifreeze protection to -34°F (-37°C). | 1-7/8 gal (with reservoir) | HR16DE |
| CVT fluid | Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid NS-3 (or equivalent). Do not mix with other fluids. Do not use ATF or manual transmission fluid. | - | HR16DE |
| Brake fluid | See Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities (P. 10-2). Use only new fluid from a sealed container. | - | all |
| Fuel | Unleaded regular gasoline with an octane rating of at least 87 AKI (Research octane number 91). Do not use E-15 or E-85. | 10-7/8 gal | all |

## Severe driving conditions

- Repeated short trips of less than 5 miles (8 km)
- Repeated short trips of less than 10 miles (16 km) with outside temperatures remaining below freezing
- Operating in hot weather in stop-and-go 'rush hour' traffic
- Extensive idling and/or low speed driving for long distances, such as police, taxi or door-to-door delivery use
- Driving in dusty conditions
- Driving on rough, muddy or salt spread roads
- Using a car-top carrier
