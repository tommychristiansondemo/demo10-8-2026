## Powertrains

- KR15DDT: 1.5L turbocharged engine with CVT; available FWD and AWD

## Oil change reminder

The vehicle is equipped with an Oil Control System (OCS) that calculates engine oil and filter change intervals based on driving conditions. Light driving conditions (freeway, city flat/moderate hills, no towing, no extended idling) result in normal display timing. Extreme conditions (repeated short trips under 5 miles, short trips under 10 miles in below-freezing temps, hot weather stop-and-go traffic, extensive idling/low-speed driving, towing/camper/car-top carrier, frequent braking/mountainous driving, sustained high speed driving) result in early display timing. If the oil replacement indicator is displayed, change oil within two weeks or less than 500 miles (800 km). If the OCS resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, change oil within 6 months or 3,750 miles (6,000 km) from last oil change. After oil change, reset the Oil Control System and oil and filter maintenance reminder.

## Severe driving conditions

- Repeated short trips of less than 5 miles (8 km)
- Repeated short trips of less than 10 miles (16 km) with outside temperatures remaining below freezing
- Operating in hot weather in stop-and-go 'rush hour' traffic
- Extensive idling and/or low speed driving for long distances, such as police, taxi or door-to-door delivery use
- Driving in dusty conditions
- Driving on rough, muddy or salt spread roads
- Using a car-top carrier
- Towing a trailer
- Using a camper
