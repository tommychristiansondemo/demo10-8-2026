## Oil change reminder

The Oil Control System (OCS) calculates engine oil and filter change intervals based on driving conditions. Under light conditions (freeway driving, city driving on flat/moderate hills, no towing or heavy loads, no extended engine idling), the OCS displays a normal change interval. Under extreme conditions (repeated short trips under 5 miles, repeated short trips under 10 miles in below-freezing temperatures, hot weather stop-and-go traffic, extensive idling/low-speed driving, car-top carrier use, frequent braking or mountainous driving, sustained high-speed driving), the OCS triggers an early change indicator. If the oil replacement indicator is displayed, change the engine oil and filter within two weeks or less than 500 miles (800 km). If the OCS resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, change the oil within six months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km) from the last oil change.

## Severe driving conditions

- Repeated short trips of less than 5 miles (8 km)
- Repeated short trips of less than 10 miles (16 km) with outside temperatures remaining below freezing
- Operating in hot weather in stop-and-go 'rush hour' traffic
- Extensive idling and/or low speed driving for long distances, such as police, taxi or door-to-door delivery use
- Driving in dusty conditions
- Driving on rough, muddy or salt spread roads
- Towing a trailer, using a camper or using a car-top carrier
