# 2025 Nissan Sentra maintenance schedule and fluid specifications

Source: 2025 Nissan Sentra Owner's Manual (USA) (https://www.nissanusa.com/content/dam/Nissan/us/manuals-and-guides/sentra/2025/2025-nissan-sentra-owner-manual.pdf), chapter 'Maintenance and schedules' and 'Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants'. Machine-extracted and independently re-verified; the Owner's Manual is authoritative.

## Powertrains

- MR20DD: 2.0L 4-cylinder

## Oil change reminder

An oil replacement indicator is displayed when an oil change is due. If the indicator is displayed, change the engine oil and filter within two weeks or less than 500 miles (800 km).

| Engine coolant | Pre-diluted Genuine NISSAN Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; 50% antifreeze / 50% water; antifreeze protection to -34°F (-37°C) | 1-7/8 gal with reservoir (manual transmission); 1-7/8 gal with reservoir (CVT) | all |
| Manual transmission gear oil | Genuine NISSAN Gear Oil NFX 75W | - | Manual transmission |
| CVT fluid | Genuine NISSAN CVT Fluid NS-3 (do not mix with other fluids) | - | CVT |

| Air conditioning system refrigerant | HFO-1234yf (R-1234yf) | - | all |
| Air conditioning system oil | Genuine NISSAN A/C System Oil Type PAG or equivalent | - | all |
| Windshield-washer fluid | Genuine NISSAN Windshield Washer Concentrate Cleaner & Antifreeze fluid or equivalent | 5/8 gal (USA) | all |

## Severe driving conditions

- Prolonged driving in dusty conditions (air cleaner filter / in-cabin microfilter)
- Severe driving conditions requiring more frequent inspection of brake pads/rotors/drums/linings, exhaust system, steering gear & linkage, axle & suspension parts, drive shaft boots (every 5,000 miles / 6 months)
- Conditions triggering more frequent brake fluid replacement (approximately every 10,000 miles / 12 months, first at 15,000 miles / 18 months)
- Conditions triggering CVT fluid replacement at 60,000 miles / 72 months
- Conditions triggering manual transmission gear oil replacement at 20,000-mile / 24-month intervals
